Patch: Keyword Fix

SCOPE OF THIS PATCH
- Keep all Alpha 0.15 menus and gameplay flow.
- Fix only keyword resolution and the existing Trample assignment panel.
- Flying and Reach attack legality.
- Protection filtering and damage prevention.
- Hexproof support for future hostile spells and abilities.
- Double Strike as two separate damage events.
- Deathtouch per positive-damage event.
- Lifelink per damage event.
- Vigilance and Haste retained.
- Indestructible survives lethal damage and Deathtouch.
- Trample stores original target group and remaining damage.
- Trample excludes original, defeated, and exiled targets.
- Trample rebuilds targets when combat state changes.
- Trample discards excess and advances if no target remains.
